# Data sources and licensing

The repository ships five deterministic replay captures, their JSON metadata,
and the known QPSK acquisition preamble under
`data/synthetic/canned_replay/`. It also ships the standalone acquisition
fixtures under `data/synthetic/acquisition/`.

All files are generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_iq.py` or
`scripts/generate_acquisition_fixtures.py`. They contain no mission RF, live
satellite traffic, operator data, or mission-specific waveform capture.

The replay fixtures cover a healthy decode, an impaired but decodable frame, a
competing acquisition peak, successful acquisition followed by CRC failure,
and a noise-only acquisition rejection. Ground-truth metadata is used for
reporting and tests only; it is not an input to candidate selection.

No claim is made that these fixtures match a specific satellite mission, air
interface, or operational channel model.
